Privacy policy

book-to-audio backup is used only by its owner, to copy their own files to their own Google Drive.

It asks Google for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it creates. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.

It runs entirely on the owner's computer. It collects no data, keeps no data anywhere else, and shares or sells nothing to anyone.
